# Physical, sound, and continuity review

The shot contract must name the force that moves an object, the medium that carries a sound, and the state that the next shot inherits. “Cinematic,” “dynamic,” or “realistic” is not a continuity explanation.

## Environment-aware sound

Start with the declared environment. In vacuum or an otherwise non-audible exterior, do not describe distant engine roar, wind, tire friction, footsteps, or a flag flapping as ordinary airborne sound. Use the actual carrier: suit or hull vibration, contact impact, internal cabin sound, radio/helmet transmission, a character's subjective perception, or non-diegetic music and design. If the story deliberately breaks realism, label that as an authorial style decision instead of hiding it in a generic ambience field.

For an audible physical event, trace source → carrier/contact coupling → actual listener or recording position. Naming a motor or writing “structure-borne sound” does not establish that a disconnected observer can hear it. If the path is absent, retain silence or explicitly identify the authorized subjective/non-diegetic treatment; do not present an added effect as physically transmitted sound.

Dust, liquid, fabric, and debris need a force source such as contact, inertia, propulsion, pressure, or impact. A flag may move from contact or inertia without an invented breeze. A fluid may expand or cling only when its pressure, viscosity, surface, or impact behavior is declared. These are prompts for review, not a demand to simulate a particular scientific model when the work has an explicit stylized rule.

## Mechanical state changes

When a deployment, assembly or transformation must read as mechanical, keep the same object and track its important components from initial position through a hinge, rail or other connection to their final position and support/lock. Explain where stored parts come from and where folded parts go; preserve attached cargo or account for its removal. Show the causally necessary changes with enough clearance and time, not a list of every imagined fastener. An insert may clarify a key operation, but a cut cannot substitute for the missing connection between two incompatible shapes. Follow the project's realism or stylization level; do not impose mechanical realism on an explicitly magical transformation.

For everyday prop actions, inspect the relevant reference for the prerequisites of contact: opening and closure, contents, occupied hands or limbs, flexible versus rigid surfaces, and what supports the load. Separate the object's design from its current state. A required open or empty state may already hold at the start; do not invent an extra opening, emptying or repositioning beat unless the story needs it. If the state changes on screen, account for the detached part or released object and carry that location into the next relevant shot. An identity reference cannot silently supply a missing action state, and a background object's structure cannot migrate onto the prop being used. These are checks of the specific action, not a requirement to enumerate every unseen component.

## Handoff ledger

For carried loads and support actions, check the continuous occupied space of the actor, grip point, whole load and attached parts. Verify upper, lower and lateral clearance, including necessary swing room; lowering the grip may clear an overhead obstacle while bringing the load into the floor. Recheck after each posture adjustment rather than proving only that the body fits. A supporting actor must be able to reach the actual contact point from the starting height and distance. Do not resolve a conflict by silently releasing the grip, shrinking the object or deforming a rigid part. These are checks against available references, not permission to invent measured dimensions or redesign a locked prop.

Track across adjacent shots: character identity and wardrobe state, positions and eyelines, prop ownership and condition, numeric UI or progress values, damage, lighting, axis, active sound bridge, and unresolved off-screen information. A shot may intentionally change a state, but the cause and the handoff must be visible. When a source asset or visual-bible revision is unknown, write `unknown` and stop inheritance rather than guessing.

The review record should separate `physical_issue`, `sound_carrier`, `continuity_state_in`, `continuity_state_out`, and `repair`. The mechanical audit can flag missing fields; a person still checks whether the proposed behavior is playable and convincing.
